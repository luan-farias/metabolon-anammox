# metabolon-anammox
Processing and statistical interpretation of microbial community composition data obtained through 16S amplicon sequencing.

Nitrogen removal is one of the main goals of landfill leachate treatment. In the classical configuration, a nitrification/denitrification sequence is applied.
An alternative is the partial nitritation/anammox process. Anammox bacteria are organisms capable to anaerobically convert ammonium directly into nitrogen gas.
The microbial community of these two treatment approaches is compared based on 16S rRNA gene amplicon data.

The experiments were conducted in the pilot plant installed in the :metabolon institute (TH Köln), located in the surroundings of the Leppe landfill, between March 2021 and August 2022.
The metagenomics analysis were conducted at the Aquatic Microbiology group at the University of Duisburg-Essen, between June 2022 and October 2023.
